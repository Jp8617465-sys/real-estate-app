## Call Script (20 min, founder-led)

### Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for the time. I'm [name], I've built a platform for AU buyers agents. Before I describe it, I want to understand your world. Specifically: the 90 days after you win a client. That OK?"

### Section 1 — Workflow reality (6 min, open-ended)
1. "Walk me through a typical matter from the day the client signs your agreement to settlement. What tools are you using?"
2. "What's the part of that 90 days that takes the most headspace?"
3. "When was the last time a deal got genuinely stressful? What happened?"
4. "Have you ever had a deal fall over or nearly fall over? What caused it?"

### Section 2 — Post-match pain probe (6 min, targeted)
5. "How do you track state-specific DD items — s66W, Form 1, Section 32?"
6. "How do you manage key dates like cooling-off end, finance clause expiry, settlement?"
7. "AUSTRAC / AML — how are you handling 100-point ID today?"
8. "How do clients know what's happening in their matter between your updates?"

### Section 3 — Tool stack (3 min)
9. "What CRM or tool are you running? What do you love about it? What do you want to change?"
10. "If a tool existed that scored deal health across your matters — on-track vs. at-risk — would you use it? What would you pay?"

### Section 4 — Close (3 min)
11. "Here's what I'm thinking of building: [30-sec Path B pitch]. What's wrong with that?"
12. "Who else should I talk to?"
13. "Can I come back to you when I have a prototype?"
