# Real client data is processed and stored only in Australia

Agreed with James on 7 November 2026 (see [Decide how consultation transcripts become reviewed records](https://github.com/Jp8617465-sys/real-estate-app/issues/54)). Real client conversations may only be sent to model hosting in Australia (Claude on AWS Bedrock Australian profiles, or a local model), and the database, stored transcripts and backups holding real client data must be hosted in Australia. Until that hosting exists, only synthetic or role-play material goes to Anthropic's Claude API. This avoids cross-border disclosure obligations rather than managing them, and gives agencies a simple promise. It constrains every hosting, provider and integration choice.
