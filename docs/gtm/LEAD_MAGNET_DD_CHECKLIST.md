## How to Use This Checklist

Every item is tagged with:

- 🚨 **BLOCKING** — must complete before exchange/settlement
- ⚠️ **CRITICAL** — high-risk if skipped
- 👤 **Responsible party** — who drives this item (solicitor, BA, broker, client, building inspector, pest inspector)
- 🏠 **Property carve-outs** — strata-only, land-excluded, etc.

Run every matter through every item. The NSW Section 66W, VIC Section 32, and QLD Form 1 specifics are where most BAs get burned at their first out-of-state engagement.

## 🌏 NSW — 22 Items

### Legal (6)

- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Contract of sale reviewed** — Solicitor. All contract terms, special conditions, and vendor disclosure.
- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Title search completed** — Solicitor. Verify title, registered owner, encumbrances via NSW LRS.
- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Easements and encumbrances checked** — Solicitor. All easements, covenants, registered interests.
- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **s66W certificate exchange** — Solicitor. NSW-specific: waive cooling-off for unconditional exchange.
- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Strata search and s184 certificate** — Solicitor. 🏠 Strata only. Financials, by-laws, levies.
- [ ] ⚠️ **Swimming pool compliance certificate** — Buyers agent. Verify current under NSW pool safety laws.

### Physical (4)

- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Building inspection completed** — Building inspector. 🏠 Not land. Licensed structural assessment.
- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Pest/termite inspection completed** — Pest inspector. 🏠 Not land. Licensed pest assessment.
- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Strata inspection report** — Solicitor. 🏠 Strata only. Common areas, maintenance, defects.
- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Pre-settlement inspection scheduled** — Buyers agent. Final walkthrough before settlement.

### Financial (6)

- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Finance approval (unconditional)** — Broker. Unconditional lender approval.
- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Deposit paid** — Client. Typically 10% to trust account by due date.
- [ ] 🚨⚠️ **Insurance arranged** — Client. Effective from exchange (risk passes at exchange in NSW).
- [ ] **Stamp duty calculated and budgeted** — Solicitor. Include FHB concessions.
- [ ] ⚠️ **Home Building Compensation Fund check** — Solicitor. NSW-specific: HBCF insurance on residential <7yrs old.
- [ ] **Water and council rates checked** — Solicitor. Outstanding amounts for settlement adjustment.

### Environmental (3)

- [ ] ⚠️ **Flood zone check** — Buyers agent. Council flood maps and historical flood data.
- [ ] ⚠️ **Bushfire zone check** — Buyers agent. BAL rating and bush fire prone land map status.
- [ ] **Contamination check** — Buyers agent. NSW EPA contaminated land record.

### Council (3)

- [ ] **Zoning verification** — Solicitor. LEP zoning aligns with intended use.
- [ ] ⚠️ **Approved vs unapproved structures** — Buyers agent. s10.7 certificate vs actual structures.
- [ ] **Planned infrastructure nearby** — Buyers agent. Council/state projects impact check.

## State-Specific Gotchas

| Gotcha | State | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| s66W certificate | NSW | Skipping it = 5-business-day cooling-off window when client wants unconditional. |
| Section 32 | VIC | Vendor statement errors can void the contract — review before signing, not after. |
| Form 1 | QLD | New seller disclosure regime. Treat as a title search equivalent. |
| HBCF | NSW | <7yr old residential builds — verify insurance exists or buyer carries the warranty risk. |
| Pool safety | QLD | Mandatory certificate. Missing = transfer delay. |
| Planning overlays | VIC | Heritage/BMO overlays change renovation economics post-settlement. |
