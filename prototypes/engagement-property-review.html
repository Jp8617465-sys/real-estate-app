Buyers-agent workflow / throwaway prototype

A reason for every property.

Review the brief, weigh the evidence, and keep the property’s story with the right client and engagement.

The question: Can an agent spot mismatches and missing evidence, choose an explained next action, and keep assessed, passed and missed properties attached to the correct engagement?

    Assumptions & public reference sources

    James named Buywell Group as the agency he is modelling for. Buywell has not participated in this project. This is an independent exploration, with no Buywell clients, internal process or endorsement.

    Working assumptions: one agent reviews a small set of candidate properties for an agreed brief. Status names, three-property review size, timing and tags are design hypotheses. Actual workload, tools, pain, value and adoption are unknown.

    James believes Buywell uses HighLevel. That remains unconfirmed; no CRM is connected or selected. Nothing here assumes HighLevel stores the property assessment record.
